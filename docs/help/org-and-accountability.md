# Titles, reporting lines and accountability

## Two titles, on purpose

Every seat carries two names and they are both correct:

| | Example | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal title** | Store Supervisor | HR, payroll, grading, contracts |
| **Comms title** | Shift Captain | How we actually talk — signatures, intros, Slack, customers |

Current comms titles include **Big Boss**, **Retail Queen**, **Marketing Girlie**, **People Person**, **Store Mum**, **Shift Captain** and **Glow Guide**.

The point of splitting them is that the playful name can change whenever we like without touching anything contractual. When Claude or the app writes something a person will read, it uses the comms title; when something formal is generated, it uses the internal one. An individual can also carry a personal comms title that differs from their seat's.

## Seats, not just people

The org chart is built from **seats** (positions), and staff are placed into them. A seat holds:

- both titles
- its reporting line (which seat it reports to)
- a one-line **purpose** — why the seat exists at all
- planned **headcount**, so vacancies are visible rather than implied

Several people can share one seat definition (four Glow Guides, one seat). A seat can sit empty, and the chart shows it as an unfilled seat rather than quietly omitting it.

## Who is my manager?

Resolved in this order:

1. An explicit manager on your staff record, if one is set (used for interim arrangements).
2. Otherwise, whoever holds the seat your seat reports to.

If that seat is **vacant**, or has **several holders**, FranHRM says so instead of guessing — a wrong answer here would put the wrong people in a 1:1.

See your own line under **Org & accountability → My line**, open anyone from **Team → their name**, or ask Claude *"who do I report to?"* Names on the chart and on a profile link to that person's full record (departments, both titles, reporting line, and — if you hold the cost or staff-edit permission — pay and statutory identity). See [Staff profiles](/help/staff-profiles).

## The accountability model

This is the part that matters most, and it has one rule:

> **Exactly one person is accountable for each outcome.** Shared accountability is no accountability.

An accountability records:

- **the outcome** — what good looks like, stated as a promise, not an activity
- **the accountable owner** — attached to a **seat** by default, so it survives someone leaving; optionally pinned to a named person
- **a metric and target** — how we know
- **a cadence** — how often it is reviewed (weekly, monthly, quarterly…)
- **contributors** — everyone else involved, marked *contributor*, *consulted* or *informed*

Contributors are deliberately separate from the owner. Plenty of people help publish the roster; one person is accountable for it going out on time.

### A title is not an accountability

Do not infer who is on the hook from someone's job title. Ask the register:

> "Who is accountable for the roster going out on time?"
> "Who owns manpower cost?"
> "What is Chloe accountable for?"

The register answers with the named person, resolved through their seat.

### When there is no owner

If an accountability's owning seat is vacant or shared, it shows as **no owner** — and that is treated as a finding, not a display quirk. The **Gaps only** filter on the accountability page lists exactly those. An outcome nobody owns is a risk you can see and assign, rather than one you discover after it goes wrong.

## Check-ins

Each accountability gets a periodic **check-in**: the metric value for the period, a status (*active* / *at risk* / *paused*), and a note. The most recent check-in drives the headline status on the register, so it reflects reality between meetings rather than whatever was true when it was created.

This is what a weekly or monthly review writes back, and it is what future meeting tooling will read to build an agenda: the owner presents, the metric is the evidence, the note is the commitment.

## Who can change what

| Action | Needs |
|---|---|
| View the chart and register | any signed-in staff member |
| Record a check-in | supervisor and above |
| Create or edit accountabilities | store manager and above |
| Create or edit seats and reporting lines | area manager and above |
