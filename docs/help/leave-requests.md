# Applying for leave

## Leave types

| Code | Type | Paid | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| AL | Annual Leave | Yes | Standard entitlement |
| MC | Medical Leave | Yes | Attach or produce your medical certificate |
| UL | Unpaid Leave | No | No balance check |
| CL | Compassionate Leave | Yes | Bereavement and family emergencies |

## Apply

1. Open **Leave**.
2. Your remaining balance per type is shown at the top.
3. Choose the **type**, the **from** and **to** dates.
4. Tick **Half day** for a half-day on a single date.
5. Add a reason (optional, but it helps your manager decide quickly).
6. Submit.

The request is created as **pending**. If you do not have enough balance for an entitled type, submission is refused up front with the number of days remaining — you will not discover the problem at approval time.

## Approval

Store managers and above approve leave. On approval the days are debited from your balance and the dates block out on the roster, so you cannot be scheduled by accident.

Pending leave also blocks roster slots. A manager who schedules you anyway sees a **leave clash** warning before publishing — see [Building and publishing a roster](/help/roster-publish).

## Cancelling

Ask your store manager. Approved leave that is cancelled has its balance credited back.
