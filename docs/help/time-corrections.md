# Fixing a missed or wrong clock

Corrections are reviewed **weekly**, so raise them as soon as you notice — do not wait for payday.

## Request a correction

1. Open **Clock** and scroll to *Missed a clock-in or out?*
2. Pick the **date**.
3. Choose what is wrong:
   - **Clock-in time** or **Clock-out time** — one time is wrong or missing
   - **Whole day missing** — you worked but there is no record at all
4. Enter the correct time in 24-hour format (e.g. `21:05`).
5. Give a short reason. "Forgot to clock out after closing" is enough.
6. Submit.

Nothing changes until a supervisor or store manager approves it.

## What your manager sees

The request shows the old value and the requested new value side by side. On approval:

- Your time entry is updated
- A correction event is appended to the clock record
- An audit entry records who approved it, when, and the before/after values

**The original value is never destroyed.** That is a compliance requirement, not a limitation.

## After payroll is locked

Once a pay period is locked for payroll, its timesheets are read-only. A correction found afterwards is posted as a **dated adjustment in the next pay period** rather than edited in place. Reopening a locked period needs an area manager or HQ admin and is logged.

See [Overtime and hours](/help/overtime-and-hours) for how corrected hours affect OT.
