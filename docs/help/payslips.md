# Payslips, sign-off and disputes

FranHRM payslips follow Singapore's itemised-payslip requirements — employer and employee, pay period, basic salary, allowances and additional payments, deductions, CPF, overtime, and net pay.

## For staff — My payslips

**My payslips** lists every payslip issued to you. Open one to see the full breakdown and **Print / PDF** it. Two actions:

- **Acknowledge** — you agree it's correct. Once you acknowledge and finance has issued it, both parties have signed off.
- **Dispute** — something's wrong. Give a reason; it's logged and finance is on the hook to resolve it.

Even after a payslip is signed off, you can still add comments — the discussion thread is a permanent record, so nothing about your pay is changed silently.

## For finance / HQ — issuing

On **Payroll** (finance and HQ only):

1. **Create** a draft: pick the person and period, enter the **monthly** basic salary, allowances, deductions, overtime and CPF. Gross and net are worked out for you. The monthly basic is **prorated automatically** for any approved **no-pay leave or sabbatical** in the period (paid leave doesn't reduce it) — the payslip notes show the proration.
2. Open the draft and **Issue** it — that's your sign-off, and the figures lock.
3. The staff member acknowledges or disputes it.
4. If disputed, discuss in the thread and **Revert to draft** to correct and re-issue.

Every create, edit, issue, acknowledge and dispute is written to the audit trail — the payroll control plane — so there's one accountable record of who did what.

## The link

Each payslip lives at its own address (`/payslip/…`). Open it and use your browser's print to save a PDF — like a shareable invoice link.
